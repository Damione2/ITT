## Taxonomy

- **M** — Established Mathematics (standard literature / proven theorem)
- **A** — ITT Axiom / Postulate (defining choice of the theory)
- **D** — Derivation / Open Mathematical Proof (active work; conditional until completed)

## Release discipline

Claims marked **D** or **P** are not to be presented as established results in the foundational paper. In particular, $k_{\min}=6$ remains conditional on completion of the explicit equivariant-lift construction, while $N_{\rm gen}=3$ remains an independent dynamical target.

The prediction manifest is the authoritative record for preregistered numerical targets. No retroactive parameter fitting is permitted in foundational modules.

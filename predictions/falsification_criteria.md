# Criteria for Falsification

1. **Non-existence of $\mathbb{Z}_6$ Equivariant Lift**: If a rigorous topological proof demonstrates that the prequantum object on $L(6,1)$ cannot admit a $\mathbb{Z}_6$-equivariant lift, $k_{\min} = 6$ selection rule is invalidated.
2. **Dynamical Instability for $N_{\rm gen} = 3$**: If the second variation of energy $\delta^2 E_Q \le 0$ for $Q=1,2,3$ or stable for $Q \ge 4$, the generational limit mechanism fails.

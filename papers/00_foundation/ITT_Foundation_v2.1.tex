\begin{abstract}
We present the minimal foundational formulation of Information-Topological Theory (ITT) as a timeless canonical gauge framework on a three-dimensional configuration space. The formulation separates standard mathematical results from ITT-specific postulates and future dynamical predictions. The standard sector contains the $SU(2)$ gauge structure on $S^3$, the canonical ITT constraint, its formal Chern--Simons state, the unit BPST topological sector, the associated Atiyah--Patodi--Singer spectral-flow construction, integer Chern--Simons level quantization, and the rational Chern--Simons invariant of the fundamental flat $SU(2)$ sector of $L(6,1)=S^3/\mathbb Z_6$. ITT then imposes an additional $\mathbb Z_6$-equivariant descent condition on the quantum line-bundle structure. When the corresponding equivariant lift is explicitly constructed, this condition gives $k\in6\mathbb Z$ and hence the minimal positive level $k_{\min}=6$. The latter is therefore presented here as an ITT selection mechanism conditional on completion of the equivariant-lift construction, not as a universal quantization condition of ordinary Chern--Simons theory. No Standard-Model parameter fitting is used in the foundational formulation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and Logical Status}

The Information-Topological Theory (ITT) is formulated as a timeless canonical gauge theory on a three-dimensional spatial manifold. The fundamental configuration space is the gauge-orbit space
\begin{equation}
\mathcal C_{\rm ITT}=\mathcal A(S^3)/\mathcal G(S^3),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal A(S^3)$ denotes the space of smooth $SU(2)$ connections on $S^3$ and $\mathcal G(S^3)$ the corresponding group of gauge transformations.

No preferred external physical time is postulated at the foundational level. A parameter used to label a family of configurations is therefore not identified with physical time.

The theory separates three logical levels:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
    \item \textbf{Standard mathematics:} gauge theory on $S^3$, BPST topology, APS spectral flow, ordinary Chern--Simons level quantization, lens-space topology, and rational Chern--Simons invariants.
    \item \textbf{ITT defining structure:} the timeless canonical constraint and the requirement of a $\mathbb Z_6$-equivariant quantum descent condition.
    \item \textbf{Open ITT results:} the explicit construction of the equivariant prequantum lift, the dynamical derivation of $N_{\rm gen}=3$, and all subsequent precision predictions.
\end{enumerate}

The central level-selection statement is therefore conditional: once the ITT $\mathbb Z_6$-equivariant lift is explicitly constructed with the character determined by the fundamental flat-sector Chern--Simons invariant, the descent condition selects $k_{\min}=6$.

\section{Canonical Configuration Space and Conventions}

\subsection{Gauge Variables}

The canonical variables are
\begin{equation}
A_i^a(x),\qquad E_a^i(x),
\end{equation}
with fundamental Poisson brackets
\begin{equation}
\{A_i^a(x),E_b^j(y)\}
=\delta_i^{\,j}\delta_b^{\,a}\delta^{(3)}(x-y),
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
\{A_i^a(x),A_j^b(y)\}=0,
\qquad
\{E_a^i(x),E_b^j(y)\}=0.
\end{equation}

The momentum $E_a^i$ is taken to be a densitized spatial vector of weight $+1$.

The $SU(2)$ generators are normalized by
\begin{equation}
T_a=\frac{\sigma_a}{2i},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(T_aT_b)=-\frac12\delta_{ab},
\qquad
[T_a,T_b]=\epsilon_{abc}T_c.
\end{equation}

The curvature is
\begin{equation}
F=dA+A\wedge A,
\end{equation}
and the magnetic density is
\begin{equation}
B_a^i=\frac12\epsilon^{ijk}F_{jk,a},
\end{equation}
where $\epsilon^{ijk}$ denotes the spatial Levi-Civita density.

\section{Chern--Simons Functional and Fundamental ITT Constraint}

Define the unnormalized Chern--Simons functional
\begin{equation}
S_{\rm CS}^{(0)}[A]
=
\int_{S^3}\operatorname{tr}
\left(
A\wedge dA+\frac23A\wedge A\wedge A
\right).
\end{equation}

With the trace convention above, its functional derivative is
\begin{equation}
\frac{\delta S_{\rm CS}^{(0)}}{\delta A_i^a(x)}
=-B_a^i(x).
\end{equation}

The fundamental ITT constraint is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathcal C_a^i
\equiv
E_a^i+\frac{k}{4\pi}B_a^i
=
E_a^i-
\frac{k}{4\pi}
\frac{\delta S_{\rm CS}^{(0)}}{\delta A_i^a}
\approx0.
}
\end{equation}

The corresponding first-order action is
\begin{equation}
S_{\rm ITT}
=
\int d\tau\int_{S^3}d^3x\,
\left[
E_a^i\frac{dA_i^a}{d\tau}
-N_i^a\mathcal C_a^i
-A_0^a\mathcal G_a
\right].
\end{equation}
Here $\tau$ is an auxiliary parameter labeling a family of configurations; it is not assumed to be physical time.

\section{Constraint Algebra}

\begin{proposition}[Commuting ITT constraints]
On the closed manifold $S^3$, the constraints
\begin{equation}
\mathcal C_a^i
=
E_a^i-
\frac{k}{4\pi}
\frac{\delta S_{\rm CS}^{(0)}}{\delta A_i^a}
\end{equation}
satisfy
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\{\mathcal C_a^i(x),\mathcal C_b^j(y)\}=0.
}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The term quadratic in functional derivatives vanishes because $\{A,A\}=0$, while $\{E,E\}=0$. The two mixed terms are opposite second functional derivatives of the same smooth functional $S_{\rm CS}^{(0)}$. Since $S^3$ is closed, no boundary contribution remains, and equality of mixed functional derivatives gives exact cancellation.
\end{proof}

The non-Abelian Bianchi identity gives
\begin{equation}
D_iB_a^i\equiv0.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{equation}
D_i\mathcal C_a^i
=
D_iE_a^i
\equiv
\mathcal G_a.
\end{equation}
Hence
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathcal G_a=D_i\mathcal C_a^i.
}
\end{equation}

\begin{remark}
At the classical level the Gauss constraint is a differential consequence of the ITT constraint. The full set is naturally described as a first-class, differentially related constraint system. At the quantum level the corresponding identity requires a fixed gauge-covariant operator ordering.
\end{remark}

For the scalar constraint we use the densitized convention
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\mathcal H_{\rm ITT}
=
\frac{1}{2\sqrt g}
\,g_{ij}\,
\mathcal C_a^i\mathcal C^{ja}.
}
\end{equation}
This definition fixes the density weight explicitly.

\section{Formal Chern--Simons State}

Canonical quantization is represented formally by
\begin{equation}
\widehat E_a^i
=
-i\frac{\delta}{\delta A_i^a}.
\end{equation}

Define
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\Psi_k[A]
=
\mathcal N
\exp\left[
i\frac{k}{4\pi}S_{\rm CS}^{(0)}[A]
\right].
}
\end{equation}

Then
\begin{equation}
\widehat E_a^i\Psi_k[A]
=
-\frac{k}{4\pi}B_a^i\Psi_k[A],
\end{equation}
so that formally
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\widehat{\mathcal C}_a^i\Psi_k[A]=0.
}
\end{equation}

Consequently, for the positive quadratic operator corresponding to the classical constraint,
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\mathcal H}_{\rm ITT}\Psi_k[A]=0
\end{equation}
formally, subject to a specified regularization and operator ordering.

This is a formal exact solution of the ITT constraint system. A complete Hilbert-space construction, functional measure, self-adjoint realization, and ultraviolet regularization are separate analytical problems and are not claimed to be solved here.

\section{Unit Topological Sector: BPST and APS}

Consider the standard $SU(2)$ BPST instanton of scale $\rho$ on flat $\mathbb R^4$, restricted to the four-ball $D_R^4$. Define
\begin{equation}
c=\frac{R^2}{R^2+\rho^2}.
\end{equation}

With the normalization above,
\begin{equation}
Q(R)
=
-\frac{1}{8\pi^2}
\int_{D_R^4}\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)
=
3c^2-2c^3
=
\frac{R^4(R^2+3\rho^2)}
{(R^2+\rho^2)^3}.
\end{equation}

On the boundary,
\begin{equation}
A|_{S^3_R}=c\,g^{-1}dg,
\end{equation}
and the Chern--Simons transgression gives, with the same orientation convention,
\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{2\pi}S_{\rm CS}(A|_{S^3_R})
=
3c^2-2c^3.
\end{equation}

Thus
\begin{equation}
\lim_{R/\rho\to\infty}Q(R)=1.
\end{equation}

Introduce the auxiliary cylinder
\begin{equation}
Y=[0,1]\times S^3
\end{equation}
with product metric and the family
\begin{equation}
A(c)=c\,g^{-1}dg.
\end{equation}

The parameter $c$ labels a path in configuration space and is not physical time. For the unit-winding interpolation, the standard APS spectral-flow correspondence identifies the net spectral flow with the unit topological index:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\operatorname{SF}(\mathcal D_{A(c)})
=
\operatorname{ind}
\left(
\mathcal D_Y^{\rm APS}
\right)
=
1.
}
\end{equation}

The statement above concerns the net spectral flow. It does not assert, without a separate spectral calculation, that exactly one individual eigenvalue crosses zero.

\section{Standard Chern--Simons Level Quantization}

For a large gauge transformation
\begin{equation}
w\in\pi_3(SU(2))\cong\mathbb Z,
\end{equation}
the normalized Chern--Simons amplitude is gauge invariant only when the level satisfies
\begin{equation}
\boxed{k\in\mathbb Z.}
\end{equation}

This is the standard Chern--Simons quantization condition. It does not select $k=6$.

\section{Lens-Space Sector}

The ITT quotient geometry is defined by the free diagonal action
\begin{equation}
(z_1,z_2)
\longmapsto
\left(
e^{2\pi i/6}z_1,
e^{2\pi i/6}z_2
\right)
\end{equation}
on $S^3\subset\mathbb C^2$. Thus
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
L(6,1)=S^3/\mathbb Z_6,
}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
\pi_1(L(6,1))\cong\mathbb Z_6.
\end{equation}

Let $\rho_1$ be the fundamental non-trivial flat representation
\begin{equation}
\rho_1(\gamma)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
e^{2\pi i/6}&0\\
0&e^{-2\pi i/6}
\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
for a chosen generator $\gamma$ of $\pi_1(L(6,1))$.

Define the normalized Chern--Simons invariant by
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{cs}(A)
=
\frac{1}{8\pi^2}
\int_{L(6,1)}
\operatorname{tr}
\left(
A\wedge dA+\frac23A\wedge A\wedge A
\right)
\pmod{\mathbb Z}.
\end{equation}

With the orientation and trace convention fixed above, the fundamental flat sector has
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\operatorname{cs}(\rho_1)
=
-\frac16\pmod{\mathbb Z}.
}
\end{equation}

One way to obtain this value is through the $(-6)$-framed surgery description of $L(6,1)$. The intersection matrix of the corresponding two-handlebody is $Q=(-6)$, and its inverse gives the rational boundary linking pairing $Q^{-1}=(-1/6)$. The reducible $SU(2)$ extension $E=L\oplus L^{-1}$ satisfies $c_2(E)=-c_1(L)^2$, producing the stated secondary invariant modulo integers.

\begin{remark}
The rational value $-1/6$ is a three-dimensional Chern--Simons invariant of a flat boundary sector. It is not being identified with an ordinary second Chern number on the closed four-manifold $L(6,1)\times S^1$. The distinction is essential.
\end{remark}

\section{ITT $\mathbb Z_6$-Equivariant Descent Condition}

The previous section determines a distinguished rational Chern--Simons invariant. ITT now adds a separate, theory-specific requirement: the quantum prequantum object must admit an equivariant lift under the chosen $\mathbb Z_6$ quotient action and descend with trivial stabilizer character.

Let $\mathcal L^k$ denote the level-$k$ prequantum Chern--Simons line-bundle object over the relevant ITT configuration space, equipped with a proposed $\mathbb Z_6$-equivariant lift. Let $\zeta$ denote the generator of $\mathbb Z_6$.

The required equivariant character is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\chi_k(\zeta)
\equiv
\exp\left(
2\pi i k\,\operatorname{cs}(\rho_1)
\right).
}
\end{equation}

Using the established value
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{cs}(\rho_1)
=
-\frac16\pmod{\mathbb Z},
\end{equation}
we obtain
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\chi_k(\zeta)=e^{-2\pi i k/6}.
}
\end{equation}

\begin{postulate}[ITT Equivariant Lift]
The prequantum Chern--Simons object used by ITT admits a $\mathbb Z_6$-equivariant lift whose stabilizer character over the fundamental flat sector is
\begin{equation}
\chi_k(\zeta)
=
\exp\left(
2\pi i k\,\operatorname{cs}(\rho_1)
\right).
\end{equation}
\end{postulate}

This is the principal open mathematical completion of the present foundation: the explicit construction of the lift, including its underlying line-bundle geometry and equivariance data, is deferred to the dedicated level-selection paper.

\section{ITT Level-Selection Rule}

For the ITT quantum object to descend to the quotient by $\mathbb Z_6$, the stabilizer must act trivially on the fiber:
\begin{equation}
\chi_k(\zeta)=1.
\end{equation}

Therefore
\begin{equation}
e^{-2\pi i k/6}=1
\end{equation}
and hence
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
k\in6\mathbb Z.
}
\end{equation}

The minimal positive ITT-compatible level is consequently
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
k_{\min}=6.
}
\end{equation}

The logical status is important:
\begin{equation}
\text{ordinary Chern--Simons theory}
\Rightarrow
k\in\mathbb Z,
\end{equation}
whereas
\begin{equation}
\text{ITT equivariant-descent postulate}
\Rightarrow
k\in6\mathbb Z.
\end{equation}

Thus $k=6$ is an ITT selection rule, not a universal theorem of ordinary $SU(2)$ Chern--Simons theory.

\section{Independent Soliton Sector}

The number of generations is not used in the level-selection argument. ITT treats
\begin{equation}
N_{\rm gen}=3
\end{equation}
as an independent dynamical hypothesis to be tested by a future soliton analysis.

The objective is to construct an ITT energy functional
\begin{equation}
E_{\rm ITT}[n,A]
\end{equation}
for which the stable topological sectors satisfy
\begin{equation}
Q_H=1,2,3,
\end{equation}
while higher-charge configurations are unstable or energetically decomposable.

Until such a stability theorem is obtained, $N_{\rm gen}=3$ remains an open ITT result and is not used to derive $k=6$.
